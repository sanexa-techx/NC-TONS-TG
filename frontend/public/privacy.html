Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Platform: NC TONs Telegram Mini App

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how NC TONs ("we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Telegram Mini App, bot, or related services. We are dedicated to respecting user privacy and minimizing data collection strictly to what is required for gaming and payout functionality.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information provided through Telegram and user interaction:

  • Telegram User Profile: Numerical Telegram User ID, first name, Telegram username, language preference, and public profile photo URL provided via the Telegram WebApp SDK during authentication.
  • TON Wallet Public Address: When you connect your wallet via TonConnect or enter a payout destination, we record your public non-custodial wallet address.
  • Gameplay & Telemetry: In-game balances (NC and TON), mining battery state, referral count, task completions, and daily check-in streak records.
  • Task Verification Proofs: Screenshots voluntarily uploaded by players to verify completion of social tasks.
Zero Sensitive Data: We NEVER ask for, collect, or store private keys, seed phrases, passwords, bank accounts, or credit card numbers. Your TON wallet remains fully non-custodial.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining your in-game mining rig state.
  • Calculating and distributing passive mining yields and game rewards.
  • Executing and verifying TON cryptocurrency withdrawal requests.
  • Preventing automated botting, multi-accounting, and ad network click-fraud.
  • Displaying live community leaderboards and referral crew statistics.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver our services, we partner with reliable infrastructure providers:

  • Telegram: Authentication and client runtime environment via the Telegram WebApp API.
  • Adsgram & Monetag: Ad delivery networks. Ad completion is verified server-to-server using anonymous user IDs to credit rewarded video bonuses.
  • TON Blockchain: Direct broadcast of on-chain withdrawal transactions.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Database and API endpoints hosted on ISO-compliant cloud servers with SSL/TLS encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention & Security

Your account records and game progress are retained as long as your account is active. We enforce strict role-based access control, cryptographic verification for Telegram initData, and secure database connection pools to protect against unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data:

  • Disconnect Wallet: You can disconnect your TON wallet at any time through the Wallet tab.
  • Account Deletion: You can discontinue use of the Mini App and request deletion of your telemetry data by contacting our team.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this policy periodically to reflect feature additions or regulatory requirements. Continued usage signifies agreement to updated terms. For privacy inquiries, contact us via Telegram: @nctons_official.
